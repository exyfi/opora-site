Opora Plus

One purchase or a monthly subscription. Plus adds:

  • your own look: the timer ring, the grove sky and the cat on the ring

The subscription renews on its own, and you can cancel it any time in your Apple ID settings.

Questions

Does Opora replace therapy?
No. Opora does not replace care from a specialist. The practices draw on CBT and DBT and help you through a hard moment, and each one explains what its steps are for.
Where is my data?
On your iPhone. Tasks, sessions and settings live on the device, Opora needs no account and shows no ads, and you can save a backup as one file. Opora checks your Plus status with the App Store and RevenueCat under an anonymous identifier, and the privacy policy has the details.
Which devices does Opora run on?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Opora has Home Screen widgets and a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
