Six questions from the WHO adult ADHD screener

The Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS v1.1) asks how often six things happened over the past 6 months. The answers take about two minutes.

The scale is a screener. Only a clinician can diagnose ADHD. Your answers stay in this browser: Google Analytics gets only the start, the finish and which of the two results came out.
